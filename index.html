How we work

One book, six stages

The same steps for every release, so you always know where your book is and what comes next.

  1. 1

    Brief & content

    You send the curriculum and your notes. We agree on pages, releases and delivery dates.

  2. 2

    Concept & character

    We build the year's idea around you: the theme, the mood, and how you appear in it.

  3. 3

    Cover

    Cover concepts first, so the series can be announced and promoted early.

  4. 4

    Interior system

    Headings, question styles, notes and dividers designed once and reused across every book.

  5. 5

    Page production

    Pages laid out chapter by chapter, with progress updates you can follow.

  6. 6

    Revisions & print files

    Agreed revision rounds, then print-ready files delivered on time.
